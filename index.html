🎬 Video Maker

Upload clips, type the narration for each scene, press Generate, then download the final video.
Your clips and texts stay in this folder after you shut down, so you can continue later.

How it works

  1. Add Scene for each part of your story. Scenes play in the order shown.
  2. Upload a clip for each scene. It is renamed automatically to Scene 1, Scene 2, Scene 3… — you don't rename files.
  3. Type Urdu or English text for that scene and pick a voice. Leave text empty if you want to keep the clip's own sound.
  4. Press Generate Video. Wait for "Done", then preview and Download.

Tip: for smooth narration, keep each scene's text to about 20 Urdu words (about 10 seconds). Longer text = the last frame will freeze while the voice finishes.
